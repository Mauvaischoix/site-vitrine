Bug Hunt

A mini game written in plain JavaScript, with no library. Catch the bugs, let the features go by.

How to play

Audio explanation (38 s). Everything that is said is written just below.

Welcome to Bug Hunt. Your mission: stop the bugs before they reach production. At the bottom of the screen, you move the "debug" bar with the arrow keys, the A and D keys, your mouse or your finger. Catch the red bugs: each bug you catch earns points, and a streak of catches raises the multiplier. Let the green diamonds go by: they are features, and catching one costs fifteen points. The coffee cup slows the game down for four seconds. If a bug reaches the bottom of the screen, you lose a life. You have three lives, and the game speeds up every twenty seconds. Press Space to pause. The game uses no sound: all the information is shown on screen. Happy hunting!

Male synthetic voice, made livelier in a cartoon style (faster and higher-pitched): Piper, “joe” voice (CC0 dataset). It is not the voice of any real famous person. Version française, avec audio en français

How it is built
  • Plain JavaScript and Canvas 2D, no library, no network calls.
  • Fixed time-step game loop: same speed on a 60 or 144 Hz screen.
  • Keyboard, mouse and touch; pauses automatically when the tab is hidden.
  • No sound: all information is visual and announced to screen readers.
  • Follows the system “reduce motion” setting and dark mode.
  • Your high score stays in your browser (local storage); nothing is sent.
