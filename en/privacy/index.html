Privacy policy

Data collected

This website stores no personal data: no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are passed on by the Web3Forms service, which emails them to me. They are used only to reply to you and are not kept by this website. See Web3Forms' privacy policy on web3forms.com.

Host

Like any host, Cloudflare processes technical connection data (IP address, browser, date) to keep the service secure and working. See Cloudflare's privacy policy on cloudflare.com.

External links

The LinkedIn link opens a third-party service with its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete data about you by writing on LinkedIn. You can also contact the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).
