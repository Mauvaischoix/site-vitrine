I

Chapter I · 2017 – 2020

Building on the ground

It all started on a scooter, in the streets of Avignon. Delivering for Domino's Pizza, I learned pace, rigour and what a waiting customer really means. Three years later, I was running the store.

  1. At first

    Delivery driver

    Keep to the clock, know every street, represent the brand on the doorstep, whatever the weather.

  2. Three years later

    Store manager

    Fifteen staff to lead, a rush-hour service to organise, orders and customers to handle without ever losing the thread.

The shop floor teaches what no school really shows you: an organisation is judged at the exact moment everything happens at once.

  • Team leadership
  • Service organisation
  • Customer relations
  • Handling peak demand
II

Chapter II · Sole trader

Starting up and structuring

I then set up my own business as an independent delivery driver, working for Amazon, La Poste, Uber Eats and Deliveroo. Being your own boss means discovering that every hour counts twice: once on the road, once in the books.

Organise

Plan routes and time slots for several clients without ever short-changing one.

Manage

Invoicing, tax returns, profitability tracking: the invisible side of the job, the one that makes it last.

Keep my word

Deadlines kept and consistent service quality, the condition for platforms to keep working with you.

Running my own business left me with a lasting reflex: think in costs, deadlines and results before even talking about a project.

  • Business creation
  • Administration
  • Scheduling
  • Self-reliance
III

Chapter III · 2025 – 2026

Leading and uniting

Area manager at AIDADOMI, a home-care network. A balancing act between care workers to support, families to reassure and a management team to report to. My role came down to one sentence: move these three worlds in the same direction.

  1. 8 months

    Two branches at once

    Sausset-les-Pins and Châteauneuf-les-Martigues, run simultaneously: 40 staff and 150 beneficiaries.

  2. Then

    Vitrolles, one of the largest in the network

    60 staff, 300 beneficiaries and revenue targets to hit, month after month.

The teams

Fair schedules, careful hiring, onboarding followed from day one to month one. Care workers who want to stay, because they are genuinely listened to.

Beneficiaries

Familiar faces, punctual visits, quick answers. At home, trust is won in the details, and lost the same way.

Management

Targets met, processes rebuilt, every file tracked in HubSpot. A branch that runs, and can be steered.

I also trained an area assistant until she was fully autonomous, and led a small admin team day to day. Passing on knowledge is part of the job: an organisation only holds if it can run without you.

  • Team management
  • Scheduling
  • Recruitment and onboarding
  • Beneficiary relations
  • Target management
  • CRM HubSpot

Chapter V · since September 2026

Exploring and innovating

Since September, I have been running a complete digital project: a website, a game, and a team of artificial intelligences working together. I am not a trained developer. I have a method: decide what needs building, hand it to the right tools, check every result.

From idea to delivery, one pair of hands.

Conceived, directed and delivered by me, with the support of artificial intelligence tools. No agency, no outside help: every decision is mine, and the AIs are the instruments.

  1. 01

    The idea

    I define the need and the expected result.

  2. 02

    Scoping

    I break the project down and set the rules and limits.

  3. 03

    Instructions

    I write each AI's instructions, according to its role.

  4. 04

    Production

    The AIs write the code, each in its own speciality.

  5. 05

    Verification

    I review, test and have security checked.

  6. 06

    The launch

    Automatic publishing, then upkeep by the AI team.
